Arduino Mega Revision 3 Specifications:

  • Dimensions: 101.5 mm L x 53.3 mm W
  • Weight: 37 g
  • Processors:
    • Main Processor: ATmega2560 16 MHz
    • USB-Serial Processor: ATmega16U2 16 MHz
  • Memory:
    • ATmega2560:
      • SRAM: 8 kB
      • Flash: 256 kB (of which 8 kB is used for the bootloader)
      • EEPROM: 4 kB
    • ATmega16U2:
      • SRAM: 512 bytes
      • Flash: 16 kB
      • EEPROM: 512 bytes
  • USB Connector: USB Type-B
  • Supported Communication Protocols:
    • Serial over USB Type-B
    • Serial over Digital Pins 0 (RX0), 1 (TX0), 19 (RX1), 18 (TX1), 17 (RX2), 16 (TX2), 15 (RX3), 14 (TX3)
    • I2C
    • SPI
    • UART
    • USART
  • Input/Output Pins:
    • 54 Digital I/O pins, 15 of them provide PWM output (pins 2-13 and 44-46)
    • 16 Analog Input Pins
  • Power:
    • Input Power Voltage Over Different Connectors:
      • 7-12 V (nominal) over Barrel Plug
      • 5 V over USB Type-B
    • I/O Voltage: 5 V
    • o DC Current per I/O Pin: 20 mA
